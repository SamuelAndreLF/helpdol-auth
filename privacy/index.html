HelpDol — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

HelpDol respects the privacy of its users and is committed to protecting the personal and financial information used while accessing its services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected or processed, how it is used, how HelpDol's integration with ChatGPT, bank connection and payment services works, which service providers may take part in that processing, and which rights are available to users.

By using HelpDol, the user acknowledges the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. About HelpDol

HelpDol is a financial management platform built primarily for self-employed workers, service providers and small businesses.

HelpDol brings ChatGPT into the user's financial management, allowing financial information to be consulted and organized using natural language.

Through this integration, the user can ask questions about their finances, review income and expenses, check balances, follow cash flow, review accounts payable and receivable, record transactions and access other financial information available in their account.

HelpDol also offers features for recording and organizing income and expenses, bank connection, importing and classifying transactions, managing accounts payable and receivable, managing collaborators, generating reports, and accepting contactless payments using technologies such as Stripe Tap to Pay, where available.

2. Information we collect and process

The information processed by HelpDol depends on the features the user uses, and may include:

  • name, email address and account-related information;
  • information required for authentication;
  • income, expenses, payments and other financial transactions;
  • balances and cash flow information;
  • accounts payable and accounts receivable;
  • information related to collaborators or contractors registered by the user;
  • receipts, documents, photographs and other information provided by the user;
  • information from bank accounts connected by the user themselves;
  • bank transactions made available through the bank integration;
  • information related to payments made and received;
  • questions, commands and requests made by the user through the ChatGPT integration;
  • information required to answer financial queries or to carry out the actions requested by the user;
  • technical information about the device, operating system and app version;
  • information required for authentication, security, fraud prevention, error diagnosis and the operation of the platform.

HelpDol seeks to limit collection and processing to the information necessary to provide, protect and improve its services.

3. Bank integration

HelpDol allows the user to connect their bank account to make it easier to organize and follow their finances.

The bank connection may be carried out using services such as Stripe Financial Connections.

When the user authorizes the connection, certain financial information made available by the financial institution may be accessed by HelpDol or by the providers responsible for the integration.

Depending on the financial institution, the permissions granted and the features used, this information may include:

  • account identification;
  • balances;
  • transactions;
  • dates and amounts of transactions;
  • transaction descriptions;
  • other financial information made available by the institution and authorized by the user.

This information may be used to organize financial transactions, follow income and expenses, calculate balances and cash flow, and provide answers to the financial questions asked by the user.

HelpDol does not ask the user to provide their bank password directly to the app.

The connection and the access to bank information depend on the user's authorization and on the services made available by the financial institution and by the providers responsible for the integration.

4. Contactless payments and receipts

HelpDol may allow eligible users to accept payments directly on compatible devices using technologies such as Stripe Tap to Pay.

With this feature, the user can request a payment and be paid by the customer tapping a compatible card or device.

The information required to authorize and process the transaction is processed by Stripe, financial institutions, card networks and the other participants required to process the payment.

HelpDol does not store full credit or debit card numbers when that information is processed directly by the providers responsible for the payment.

After a transaction, HelpDol may receive the information needed to identify and record the payment in the user's financial management, such as amount, date, transaction status and other information made available by the payment processor.

Payments are also subject to the terms and privacy policies of the respective providers.

5. Integration with ChatGPT and OpenAI

HelpDol uses ChatGPT as part of its financial management experience, allowing the user to interact with their information using natural language.

Through this integration, the user can ask questions about their financial situation and review income, expenses, balances, cash flow, accounts payable, accounts receivable and other information available in their account.

The user may also request actions supported by HelpDol, such as recording income, recording expenses, recording payments to collaborators, and performing other operations made available by the platform.

In order to answer requests or carry out an action, the necessary information may be processed by the systems involved in the integration.

That information may include the text or command sent by the user and the data required to answer the question or perform the requested operation.

For example, when the user asks about their cash flow, accounts payable or income, HelpDol may use the corresponding financial information available in the account to produce the requested answer.

When the user requests that income or an expense be recorded, the information provided in the request may be used to create the corresponding record in HelpDol.

HelpDol seeks to limit processing and sharing to the information necessary to fulfill the user's request.

The artificial intelligence features used in this integration may be provided by OpenAI, responsible for ChatGPT, and the processing carried out by those services may also be subject to OpenAI's applicable terms and privacy policies.

6. How we use the information

The information processed by HelpDol may be used to:

  • create, authenticate and administer the user's account;
  • provide the financial management services;
  • record and organize income and expenses;
  • calculate and present balances and cash flow;
  • manage accounts payable and accounts receivable;
  • organize information coming from the bank connection;
  • classify and present financial transactions;
  • answer questions asked through the ChatGPT integration;
  • carry out financial actions requested by the user;
  • record payments to collaborators;
  • process or facilitate payments made and received;
  • record payments received;
  • generate receipts, documents and reports;
  • send notifications and reminders;
  • provide customer service and support;
  • protect accounts and prevent fraud;
  • diagnose errors and fix failures;
  • improve the security and the operation of the platform;
  • comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

7. Third-party services

To provide its services, HelpDol may use third-party technology and service providers. These may include:

  • OpenAI / ChatGPT — provision of the artificial intelligence features used in the natural language interaction and in the functionality integrated with ChatGPT.
  • Stripe — payment processing, bank connection through Stripe Financial Connections, Stripe Tap to Pay and other financial services used by HelpDol.
  • Supabase — infrastructure, database, authentication and backend services used by the platform.
  • Firebase / Google — technical resources related to the operation of the app, notifications, security, diagnostics and other services implemented by HelpDol.
  • RevenueCat — management and validation of subscriptions and in-app purchases, where used.
  • Apple — authentication, app distribution, purchases and other services related to the iOS ecosystem.
  • Google / Google Play — authentication, app distribution, purchases and other services related to the Android ecosystem.

These providers may process the information required to deliver their respective services and have their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Subscriptions and purchases

When a subscription or purchase is made through the App Store or Google Play, the payment may be processed directly by Apple or by Google, depending on the platform used.

HelpDol may receive the information needed to identify the purchase and verify the subscription status, but does not receive the full card details used for the payment when those details are processed directly by the store.

HelpDol may also use services such as RevenueCat to manage and validate subscriptions.

9. Sharing of information

HelpDol does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers when this is necessary to:

  • operate HelpDol;
  • answer requests made through ChatGPT;
  • carry out actions requested by the user;
  • perform authorized bank connections;
  • process payments made and received;
  • manage subscriptions;
  • provide infrastructure, authentication and security;
  • detect or prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorized activity;
  • comply with legal obligations or legally valid requests;
  • protect the rights and safety of HelpDol and its users.

Sharing is limited to the information necessary for the corresponding purpose.

10. Information security

HelpDol uses technical and administrative measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration or improper disclosure.

HelpDol also uses specialized providers for certain infrastructure, authentication, bank connection, payment and technological processing functions.

However, no system connected to the internet and no method of electronic storage can guarantee absolute security.

The user is also responsible for protecting their credentials and the devices used to access their account.
